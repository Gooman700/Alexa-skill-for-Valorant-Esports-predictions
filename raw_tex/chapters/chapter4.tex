\section{Risk Analysis}
The success of this project is contingent upon the proactive management of technical, operational, and scope-related risks. To address these, a Preliminary Hazard Analysis (PHA) was conducted, allowing for the identification of potential accident events, their causes, and the implementation of mitigation strategies to reduce the residual risk score to an acceptable level.
