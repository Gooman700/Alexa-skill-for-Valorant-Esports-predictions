\chapter*{\Large \centering Abstract}

Valorant is a five versus five first-person tactical hero shooter video game developed by Riot Games. The competitive Esports scene for Valorant, the Valorant Champions Tour (VCT), has grown rapidly since its inaugural tour in 2021, attracting millions of followers worldwide. Currently, there is a severe lack of tools available which display predictions for the upcoming VCT matches, and in addition, the ones that do exist are websites that are unintuitive to use and littered with pop-ups and adverts, all contributing towards a poor user experience.

This report presents a machine learning approach to forecasting VCT match results, integrating historical game data and player statistics with a well-chosen training and testing algorithm to produce accurate results. The predictive tool is embedded within an Amazon Alexa skill to bring it to life through speech-powered technology, providing a sleek and accessible user experience.
