\chapter*{\Large \centering Abstract}

Valorant is a five versus five first-person tactical hero shooter video game developed by Riot Games. The competitive Esports scene for Valorant, the Valorant Champions Tour (VCT), has grown rapidly since its inaugural tour in 2021, attracting millions of followers worldwide. Currently, there is a severe lack of tools available which display predictions for the upcoming VCT matches, and in addition, the ones that do exist are websites that are unintuitive to use and littered with pop-ups and adverts, all contributing towards a poor user experience.

This report presents a machine learning approach to forecasting VCT match results, integrating historical game data and player statistics with a well-chosen training and testing algorithm. The final tool will be quick and easy to access and use, making use of Amazon's Alexa to bring it to life through speech-powered technology, and it will produce highly accurate results.

To date, a dataset of past VCT matches from 2021 has been researched and reviewed, with a large quantity of features per data point. The key differences between Amazon Alexa and Google Home have been discussed and compared, resulting in Alexa being chosen. Development environments for Alexa have been explored, and Python has been chosen as the primary programming language. A risk assessment has been produced, taking into account a variety of risks ranging from likely to very unlikely.
